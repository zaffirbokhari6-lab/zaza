Hawa privacy policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

Hawa provides air-quality estimates and health guidance for places users choose. This policy describes information used by the Hawa app and website.

Location and searches

If you choose “Use my location,” Hawa asks your device for your location and sends the coordinates to Open-Meteo to retrieve an air-quality estimate. If you search for a place, the search text is sent to Photon/OpenStreetMap or Open-Meteo Geocoding to find its coordinates. Location is used to provide the feature you requested. Hawa does not operate a user account or its own server that stores your location.

Saved areas

Saved areas are kept in local storage on your device so you can reopen them. They are not uploaded to a Hawa account. You can remove saved areas in the app or clear the app’s website data in your browser settings.

Service providers

Hawa relies on Open-Meteo for air-quality data and geocoding, and Photon/OpenStreetMap for place search. Those providers receive the requests needed to provide the feature, which may include your network address and the location or search term in the request. Their own privacy policies and service terms apply to their processing.

Analytics, advertising, and sale

Hawa does not include advertising, cross-app tracking, or an analytics SDK, and does not sell personal information.

Children and contact

Hawa is a general-purpose utility and does not request a name, account, or contact details. For privacy questions, use the project support page.
